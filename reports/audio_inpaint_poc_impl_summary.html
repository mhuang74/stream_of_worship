Branch audio_imprint_poc (name kept from the earlier imprint prototype), pushed to origin. Commits: e194d849 (implementation), 25f7a973 (run record + GPU accounting), a4ffb2d1 (review page), 4242a56e (review-page caveat).

Goal: make a seam between two songs sound continuous by generating only the music that is missing, while every second of real recording stays sample-identical in the output. Two variations share one script: --mode bridge (an 8 s or 16 s replacement bridge) and --mode gap (the production 2-beat gap, 1.393 s), each runnable on ACE-Step 1.5 base, ACE-Step turbo, or Stable Audio 3 (--engine).

2. How the pipeline works now

  • Montage = arranged_audio() of each side (all arrangement steps in playback order), capped by --context-seconds (default 15 s per side; 0 = whole arrangement), plus a hole of digital silence where the seam is. In gap mode the hole is exactly two beats at the incoming recording's tempo (1.393 s = 61,431 frames at 86.13281 BPM).
  • One request per take, mask over the whole hole: ACE-Step gets repaint_start/repaint_end, duration, recording-sourced bpm/keyscale, 64 steps + ADG + conservative repaint, and a 30 s / 48 kHz style reference built from both arranged sides. SA3 gets exactly its six keys (prompt, seed, steps, start_seconds, end_seconds, duration_seconds) and no reference.
  • Assembly never splices model audio into the context: the clip's two 15 s sides are the recordings themselves (asserted sample-for-sample in tests), the model contributes only the hole.
  • Level continuity is anchored on the joints, not on means: each end of the hole is gain-ramped to the 200 ms of real audio beside it, capped at EDGE_GAIN_LIMIT_DB = 18.0; the residual is recorded as staged_edge_error_db.
  • A dead head is covered with real audio: Seam.a_post carries A's own audio from the seam up to HEAD_PAD_MAX_SECONDS = 3.0; assemble splices it over the hole's measured dead head (+0.15 s margin, 0.25 s crossfade), reported as hole_head_pad_seconds.
  • The tail is handed to B: a 0.5 s equal-power crossfade from the hole into B's real opening (hole_overlap_seconds), measured before and after as raw_join_rms_jump_db vs join_rms_jump_db.
  • Refusals instead of silent defects: a hole that is non-finite, peaks below 0.001, or sits below SILENCE_RMS_FLOOR_DB = -50.0 is refused; a hole that cannot be staged within MAX_JOIN_RESIDUAL_DB = 6.0 after the 18 dB ramp is refused; in gap mode a fill with more than MAX_GAP_TRAILING_SILENCE_MS = 50.0 of dead air before the incoming downbeat is refused. Every refusal is written as status: "failed" with the measured numbers, so the listening page shows the failure rather than a dropout.
  • Gap timing corrections (copied from gen_seam_fill_run3.py): raw (model output), trim (drop the lead-in, pad the end), grid (also time-stretch so hit two lands one incoming beat after hit one). detect_onsets dedupes hits closer than 60 ms, because librosa split one attack into 23.22 ms and 46.44 ms and reported a −650 ms beat-two error on a fill whose accent was on the beat.

Verdict: ACE-Step base fills the gap; SA3 medium does not (2 fills came back at −66 dB RMS silence, 9 could not be staged with a 17–42 dB residual after the 18 dB ramp, 1 left 58 ms of dead air). On bridges SA3 agrees with A's harmony far better (0.65 vs 0.37) and is 9× faster, while base has the more coherent stereo image and better-behaved raw tail.

For a gap the right join is expected to be positive (the fill's last hit decays into B's downbeat): join_rms_jump_db.right reads −0.4 … +23.9 dB on the ok rows. The gap gate is dead air (>50 ms refused) and staging residue, not the step.

Two measurement defects were found and fixed on the way:

  • SA3's app clamped every generation to its model default window (5_292_032 frames = 120 s at 44.1 kHz), so a 331.8 s montage came back short and the hole fell in fit_length's zero padding; the app now passes sample_size and refuses durations above MAX_SECONDS. Feeding the whole arrangement to SA3 also made it fade its hole in from digital silence, which is why the context is bounded to 15 s per side.
  • main() summed GPU time over records, and a gap take copies one call's meta onto its three correction rows; it now sums over calls (45.0 s instead of 134.9 s for run5-gap).

7. Known limits / not done

  • SA3 medium is not a usable gap engine at this geometry; the run is kept so the page shows the failure instead of hiding it. sa3-small-music (used by run2/run3 with the same 1.393 s gap) is the untested alternative.
  • Bridge holes are 8 s and 16 s only (LENGTHS = (8, 16)); --lengths 30 is untested.
  • Realism is a listening judgement; the automated gates only prove the geometry, the sample-exact context, the staging and the refusals.
  • The scoring rubric stores nothing: typed scores are lost on reload, and the hub page is read-only (no per-take scores).
